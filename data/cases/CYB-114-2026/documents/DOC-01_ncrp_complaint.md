National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal - Acknowledgement No. 31510260004417, registered on 06-Oct-2026 at 09:42 hrs through the 1930 helpline. Category: Online Financial Fraud - Impersonation of Government Officials ("digital arrest").

Complainant: Prof. Suresh Kulkarni, age 67, retired professor, residing at Flat 6, Saraswati Apartments, Karve Road, Kothrud, Pune 411038. Mobile number 9822041763.

Summary as given by the complainant: On 03-Oct-2026 at about 10:12 hrs I received a call from 7303518246 from a person claiming to be from "SwiftAir Courier". He said a parcel booked on my Aadhaar to Taiwan contained 400 grams of MDMA, passports and credit cards. He transferred the call to a person who said he was from Mumbai Police Cyber Cell, who then asked me to join a video call on the ConnectX app with "CBI officer Vikram Rathore".

The person in police uniform on the video call, using number 8969204117, said I was under "digital arrest" and must not tell anyone, including my family. The 'digital arrest' continued through Sunday 04-Oct. He said my bank funds must be verified by the RBI and would be returned within 48 hours after verification.

Under fear I made three RTGS transfers from my Sahyadri National Bank account to an account in the name of Rohit Pawar at Konkan Small Finance Bank: Rs 9,50,000 and Rs 12,00,000 on 03-Oct and Rs 17,00,000 on 05-Oct. Total loss Rs 38,50,000.

On 06-Oct morning when the callers stopped responding, I told my wife and we called 1930. Helpline action taken: transaction details forwarded to Konkan Small Finance Bank for lien/freeze at 09:58 hrs on 06-Oct-2026.
