Statement recorded by PI Meera Deshpande at Cyber Police Station on 07-Oct-2026. I, Suresh Kulkarni, age 67, retired professor of physics, residing at Kothrud, Pune, state as follows.

I live with my wife Vandana. Our son lives in Canada. My nephew Anil Kulkarni, who works in Bengaluru, stayed with us from 20-Sep to 28-Sep-2026 and helped me set up net banking on my phone. He knows I keep my savings in Sahyadri National Bank.

On 03-Oct at about 10:12 hrs I got a call from 7303518246. The caller said he was from SwiftAir Courier and that a parcel in my name with drugs had been stopped at Mumbai customs. He gave me a "complaint number" and transferred the call.

The next person, who said he was Inspector Patil of Mumbai Police Cyber Cell, said my Aadhaar was linked to a money-laundering case. He asked me to install the ConnectX app and join a video call. On the video call a man in a police uniform with a CBI logo behind him introduced himself as Vikram Rathore, CBI. He showed me an "arrest warrant" with a Supreme Court seal.

Vikram Rathore told me I was under digital arrest, that I must keep the camera on at all times and must not tell anyone, including my wife, or I would be arrested immediately. He spoke Hindi with a slight Marathi accent. He called me many times from 8969204117 on ConnectX calls.

He said all my funds must be sent to an "RBI-supervised safe account" for verification. As instructed, I went to my bank and sent RTGS of Rs 9,50,000 at about 15:40 and Rs 12,00,000 at about 17:05 on 03-Oct, and Rs 17,00,000 at about 11:20 on Monday 05-Oct, after being kept on video watch through Sunday 04-Oct. The beneficiary name was Rohit Pawar, Konkan Small Finance Bank. I was told this was an RBI nodal officer's account.

I did not share my net-banking password with anyone. I made all transfers myself at the branch under instructions on the video call. Nobody else had my phone between 03 and 05 Oct.

On 06-Oct the numbers stopped answering. I realised I had been cheated and informed my wife, who called 1930. I can identify the voice of Vikram Rathore if heard again.
