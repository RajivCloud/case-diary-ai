Observation note prepared by PI Meera Deshpande on 16-Oct-2026 after viewing the CCTV footage of Narmada Bank ATM NB-NSK-0417, Dwarka Circle, Nashik, for 05-Oct-2026, received on a pen drive from Narmada Bank on 10-Oct-2026.

At 14:30:52 hrs (camera timestamp) a man aged about 27 to 30, with a short beard, wearing a white shirt and a black cap, enters the ATM cabin. Between 14:31 and 14:44 hrs he makes six withdrawals and counts the cash. He leaves at 14:45:10 hrs on a black two-wheeler.

The face of the man is clearly visible at 14:38 hrs when he removes his cap. The face resembles the photograph of Imran Rafiq Shaikh on his Narmada Bank KYC record. The camera clock was checked against the bank's transaction log and found to be about 40 seconds ahead.

Note: the footage is relied upon for identification of the accused. The Section 63 BSA certificate for this footage has not yet been received from the bank.
