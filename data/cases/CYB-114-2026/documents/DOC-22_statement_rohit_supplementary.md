Supplementary statement recorded by PI Meera Deshpande on 14-Oct-2026 at Cyber Police Station, after the call records and the bank's statement were shown to Rohit Pawar.

I, Rohit Pawar, now state that what I said on 11-Oct was not fully true. In September a friend from my college introduced me to a man called Imran bhai from Malegaon. He said he needed accounts for his mobile business and would pay Rs 15,000 per month for using my account.

I agreed because I needed money for fees. On 25-Sep Imran bhai came with me to the bank to start net banking and increase the limit. On 27-Sep he sent me Rs 15,000 on UPI.

On 03-Oct and 05-Oct I got OTP messages from my bank. Imran bhai had told me to forward every OTP immediately to a number he gave me, which was 8969204117. I forwarded the OTPs as SMS. I did not know the money was from a fraud on an old man. I never met or spoke to anyone named Sameer.

I am ready to give this statement before a Magistrate.
